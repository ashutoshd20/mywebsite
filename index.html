About

Four years of backend, machine learning and research work, most of it close to physics experiments.

I am a software engineer who moves between systems work and research. At CERN, I maintained the production database that tracks how the CMS Central Tracker is assembled, and built the services and web tools people use to read and manage that data.

My research started with a simple question: do language models know that etiquette differs by region? That became EtiCor, a corpus of etiquette across cultures, and later a set of metrics for measuring etiquette bias in LLMs. Both were published.

I studied Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kanpur with minors in AI and Computer Systems, which is why I am equally comfortable with a Verilog processor and a PyTorch training run.

Publications

Language models and culture

ACL 2025 FindingsVienna

Towards Understanding Etiquettical Bias in LLMs

  • Proposed new evaluation tasks and seven algorithmic metrics for measuring etiquettical bias in LLMs.
  • Built the metrics from different vectors of LLMs to capture their generative behavior.
  • Ran experiments on five popular LLMs and found clear regional preferences in social norms.
EMNLP 2023Singapore

EtiCor: Corpus for Analyzing LLMs for Etiquettes

  • Curated a new dataset of etiquettes from several cultures.
  • Probed LLMs for accuracy and bias across different aspects of etiquette, and defined general alignments.
  • Fine-tuned models to account for regional biases, making them more culturally aware and accurate.

Particle physics with the CMS collaboration

CMS collaborationAugust 2025

Operation and performance of the CMS silicon strip tracker with proton-proton collisions at the CERN LHC

  • Evaluated tracker performance during LHC Run 2 using detector observables.
  • Studied radiation effects in silicon sensors and optical links, comparing measurements with simulation for longevity studies.
  • Characterized saturation of the APV25 preamplifier and its effect on tracker performance, supporting calibration work.
CMS collaborationNIM A 2026

Evaluation of 3D pixel silicon sensors for the CMS Phase-2 Inner Tracker

  • Validated 3D silicon pixel sensors for the Phase-2 tracker upgrade in beam tests, before and after irradiation.
  • Measured hit efficiency, channel masking and radiation tolerance using RD53A and CROCv1 readout chips.
  • Characterized sensor operation under the extreme radiation of the LHC, supporting qualification of future tracking detectors.

I have also contributed to more than five other papers that are under peer review. CMS papers are published under the collaboration author list.
